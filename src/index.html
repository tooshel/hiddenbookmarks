The reader behind the bookmarks

I’m a romance reader with a phone full of ARCs and a heart full of book boyfriends. The Hidden Bookmarks is where I tell people exactly why they need to read your book next.

My reviews are honest, because readers can tell when they aren’t. I’m usually listening to Taylor Swift or reading an ARC, and sometimes both.
