# Home (hiddenbookmarks.com)

Scope: the one-page business site. Mode: **Persuade**. Romance authors, their
assistants, and PR companies decide whether to send her a book, then email her.
Proof: her six latest posts (graphics plus real review excerpts) and the four
kinds of work they show. Constraints: static GitHub Pages, no forms, no invented
stats, prices, or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a mass-market romance paperback with a die-cut step-back
cover. Scrolling swings the front cover open on its hinge to reveal her latest
six books inside, which acts out the cover reveals she does. It refuses the
cream-and-serif "book blog" and the dark-moody bookstagram grid.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched raspberry cover field, aubergine step-back interior, gold
foil reserved for the script title, rules, and window bevel. Pink-white text
(never cream). Marigold is reserved by law for anything you can act on (the
palette law raise from the arcade challenger). Ballet script in foil, Literata
Black for paperback-scale headings, condensed Archivo caps for spine and back
cover labels. Inset foil frame, publisher roundel, sticker flash, barcode spine.

STORY: The visitor sees a romance promoter with taste, sees her real work, learns
the four services, and emails her about their book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed raspberry cover in an inset gold frame. Left 55%: huge
foil script "The Hidden Bookmarks", italic tagline, service line, marigold "Send
me your book" button. Right: a tall arched die-cut window (gold bevel) showing
the fanned graphics behind it. Marigold sticker flash top-right, roundel seal
bottom-left, "Open the cover" cue at the bottom. On mobile everything stacks and
the arch sits below the button.

FORM: Mass-market romance step-back paperback, #4 of 7 on my ordered list, seed
d643b593. Raise from the Scher street-poster challenger: poster-scale type
commitment and one color held for action. Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed
3D hinge of the front cover (rotateY around the left spine) revealing the
interior; with reduced motion or no JS the cover simply sits above the interior.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
